Our Story

Welcome to Namhto Foods, We are passionate about providing our customers with delicious and nutritious yogurt products that promote a healthy lifestyle. Our journey began with a vision to create a brand that not only offers high-quality yogurt but also contributes positively to the well-being of our consumers.

Our Vision

To build one of Africa's leading nutrition companies, improving everyday nutrition through accessible, innovative and locally manufactured food products.

Our Mission

To make better nutrition accessible to African families by producing high-quality, affordable and innovative dairy-based and nutrition products that combine great taste, convenience and nutritional value.

Now every spoonful of our yogurt is a promise kept; a delicious step towards empowering our farmers, nourishing our families, and nurturing our nation, together.

Making a Difference

We're committed to creating positive change in our communities and environment

Our Pledge to Local Sourcing

We are committed to Nigeria's dairy independence. We are investing in local farmers and modern practices to build a robust, homegrown dairy supply chain.

Creating Entrepreneurial Hubs

By integrating smallholder farmers into our core business model, we're turning local communities into vibrant entrepreneurial hubs. Our focus on training and supporting Nigerian youth in agriculture provides not just jobs, but sustainable careers.

Empowering Women in Dairy

Through our dedicated partner program, we provide training, resources, and guaranteed access to a reliable market. This ensures stable income for women farmers, enabling them to invest in their families and futures.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our products

Are your yogurts organic?

Yes! All our yogurts are made with certified organic ingredients. We source from organic farms that do not use synthetic pesticides or fertilizers.

How many days after opening should I consume my yogurt?

Once opened, it will stay at peak quality for 5-7 days if stored properly. But generally, it will remain safe to consume for at least 2 weeks.

What is so special about Greek yogurt?

It has 2x more protein and it is more nutrient-dense than regular yogurt. It is also thicker and creamier.

Can I order from you directly and do you process bulk orders?

Yes, and yes we do process bulk orders. Contact us for more details about bulk ordering and direct purchases.
